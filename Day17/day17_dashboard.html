  • ✅ Strong for long highway hauls
  • ✅ Decent mileage (~19.6 km/l)
  • ❌ Highest maintenance/km (₹1.00)
  • ❌ Higher CO₂ than EV/E85

  • ✅ Widely available, fast refuel

🚗 Best for: flex-fuel loyalists chasing lower emissions, not lower bills
