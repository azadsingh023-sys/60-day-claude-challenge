Day 33: Media Integrity Analyzer

Goal: Build an interactive single-file web app teaching media literacy through guided discovery rather than testing prior knowledge.

What I built:

Single-file HTML/CSS/JS app (no frameworks, no APIs, fully offline)
Challenge 1 — Headline Detective: fictional headline/article pairs, click-intent question, mismatch-tagging, accuracy scoring, and rewrite reveal
Challenge 2 — Emotion Detector: fictional social post, feeling identification, manipulation-technique reveal, neutral rewrite
Live Media Integrity metrics (Headline Accuracy, Source Reliability, Emotional Manipulation, Audience Targeting)
Final Dashboard: overall score, key learnings, biggest red flag, 3 practical habits, replay with new scenarios
Premium dark editorial UI with animations, progress tracker, hover states, responsive layout

Tech: Vanilla HTML/CSS/JS only — zero dependencies

Learning takeaway: Prompted for instructional design (concept → why it matters → interactive challenge → reveal) rather than just app functionality — reinforced that AI can architect a teaching flow, not just UI.
