Day 32: Think Like a Marketing Strategist

Interactive single-file HTML/React app that teaches marketing strategy through a replayable simulation rather than static content generation.

Flow: Choose mode (own business / personal brand / random client) → audience breakdown → platform fit analysis → pick 3 content pillars → 30-day weekly roadmap → random curveball event with branching consequences → final Growth Report.

Highlights:

Fully offline — React, ReactDOM, and Babel vendored inline, no CDN/backend dependency
Dual modes: business branding vs. personal branding (different platforms, pillars, and roadmap logic for each)
"What is this? / Why does it matter?" teaching boxes throughout
"How to Ask Claude" reusable prompt cards after every section — teaches prompt engineering alongside marketing
Randomized business/persona generator for endless replayability

Stack: React (CDN-free, inlined UMD builds) + Babel standalone, vanilla CSS, no npm/build step.
