Day 18 — Meeting Transcript → Action Dashboard

What I built: Tested Claude's ability to convert a raw meeting transcript into a structured, interactive HTML dashboard (Notion/Linear-style UI).

Key learnings:

Fed a sample quarterly strategy meeting transcript to Claude
Claude auto-extracted: Summary, Key Takeaways, Action Items (with Owner/Deadline/Status), Open Questions, Risks/Blockers, and Conflicts
Used "Transcript Mode" to get a Speaker-wise breakdown — decisions and action items attributed to each speaker
Nothing was invented — missing info was clearly marked "Not specified"

Takeaway: Great real-world use case for turning meeting chaos into actionable clarity — useful for ITSM/incident review meetings too.
