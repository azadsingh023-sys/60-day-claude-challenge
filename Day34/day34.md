Day 34: Marketing Detective 🕵️‍♂️

What I built: A gamified, single-file HTML/CSS/vanilla-JS app that turns marketing case-study analysis into a detective investigation.

Features:

10 detailed fictional marketing cases (industry, objective, audience, channels, budget split, metrics, comments, social performance)
Interactive corkboard with draggable/clickable evidence cards + sticky-note clues
4-option "solve the case" mechanic with correct/incorrect feedback
Animated "Case Closed" stamp + confetti transition
Auto-generated Learning Report with animated metric bar charts, root-cause explanation, and suggested improvements
Dark, premium "detective noir" UI theme (crimson/gold palette, paper textures, push pins)

Tech: Pure HTML/CSS/vanilla JavaScript — no frameworks, no build step, runs as a standalone local file.

Why it matters: Reframes passive case-study reading as active investigation — forces pattern recognition across metrics, customer sentiment, and clues before revealing the root cause.
