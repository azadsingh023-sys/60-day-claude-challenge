Day 31: AI Supply Chain Control Tower

Built a browser-based operations simulation game where the player acts as Head of Operations for a global supply chain, triaging randomly generated disruption alerts (port congestion, supplier delays, truck breakdowns, warehouse stockouts, customs holds, demand spikes, machine failures, weather disruptions, inventory count errors, damaged shipments) under a 3-minute timer.

Key features:

8 live KPIs (Service Level, Customer Satisfaction, Inventory Health, Transportation Efficiency, Operating Cost, Revenue Protected, Score, Remaining Time)
Priority-based alert countdowns with auto-escalation penalties if ignored
Multiple action choices per alert, each with distinct KPI/score consequences, including delayed follow-up effects
Difficulty modes (Calm Seas / Standard Ops / Crisis Mode) affecting alert frequency
Live scrolling event log, pause/resume, sound toggle, help modal
End-of-game performance grading (A+ to D) with a full KPI/stat summary and Play Again flow

Stack: Single self-contained HTML file — vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript only. No frameworks, libraries, or backend.
