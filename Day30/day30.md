Day 30: Supply Chain Builder

What I built: A single-file, offline-capable React app (via CDN + Babel) that teaches supply chain fundamentals through interactive decision-making. Users generate a random company, then choose suppliers, factory location, warehouse strategy, transport mode, and inventory strategy — with plain-English explanations of each concept and trade-off before every choice.

Key features:

Randomized company generator (industry, product, countries served, demand level)
5 live-updating business metrics: Cost, Delivery Speed, Risk, Customer Satisfaction, Sustainability
Animated progress bars, smooth transitions, premium dark-theme dashboard UI
Final scored dashboard (0–100) with strengths, weaknesses, biggest risk, and 3 practical improvements
Fully responsive, no backend/APIs — runs by just opening the HTML file

Milestone: 🎯 30/60 days complete — halfway through the challenge!
