Day 29: Operation Lifeline — Supply Chain Crisis Lab

A single-file React (CDN) simulation of a supply chain crisis, built end-to-end with Claude.

What it does:

Randomly generates a fictional company (industry, revenue, factories, suppliers, etc.) and a random crisis event
War Room: pick 3 of 6 response actions, see animated impact on Cost/Inventory/Profit/Delivery/Satisfaction
4-round branching supplier negotiation (Trust, Price, Lead Time)
5-question CEO boardroom leadership assessment
AI investment selection (pick 2 of 5 capabilities) with projected business impact
Final dashboard: 0–100 overall score across 6 dimensions, plus biggest mistake, best decision, expert recommendation, and lessons learned

Stack: Plain HTML/CSS/JS, React + Babel via CDN — no build step, runs fully offline.

Why it matters: Turns abstract supply-chain-risk concepts into a playable, replayable decision exercise where every choice has a visible trade-off.
