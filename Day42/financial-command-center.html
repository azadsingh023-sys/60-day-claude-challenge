Financial Tips

Automate your SIPs the day after salary credit — "pay yourself first" beats leftover-based saving.
Keep EMIs (including home loan) under 40% of take-home income to stay financially flexible.
Review and cancel unused subscriptions every quarter — small recurring costs compound over years.
Maintain 6 months of expenses in a liquid emergency fund before aggressive investing.
Increase your SIP amount by 10% every year in line with salary hikes (step-up SIP).
Separate insurance from investment — term insurance + mutual funds usually beats ULIPs.
