Today's build: NutriScope — an AI-assisted nutrition analytics app that logs food intake, calculates calorie/macro/micronutrient targets, and flags deficiencies & excesses in real time.

Started with an MVP (profile inputs, food logging, dashboard) and then enhanced it further — CSV upload, 60-food database, a 2-day meal planner, and rule-based risk analysis.

Key learning today: Claude is genuinely great at turning a messy requirements list into a structured, working single-file app — but the real skill is in writing clear, layered prompts (MVP first, enhance next) instead of dumping everything into one giant ask.

Also learned: good UI/UX + accurate data assumptions matter as much as the logic itself.
