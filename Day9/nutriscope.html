Educational Disclaimer

NutriScope is a self-tracking educational tool intended to help you understand general nutrition patterns. Nutrient values are approximate averages for common preparations and can vary with brand, recipe, and cooking method. Target values are derived from widely published adult dietary reference ranges and are not individualized medical prescriptions. This tool does not replace consultation with a registered dietitian, physician, or other qualified healthcare provider — especially if you are pregnant, managing a health condition, or under medical supervision.

Nutrition data reference basis: Approximate per-100g composition values compiled from generally published nutrition references, including national food composition tables for Indian foods (ICMR–National Institute of Nutrition) and international food composition data (USDA FoodData Central), rounded for educational estimation purposes.

Target reference basis: Adult macro/micronutrient reference ranges follow commonly published dietary reference intake conventions; individual needs vary by health status, pregnancy/lactation, and clinical guidance.
