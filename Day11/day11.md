## 📅 Day 11/60 — ATS Resume Optimizer & Resume Generator

**Goal:** Use Claude to align an existing resume with a target job description — without inventing any experience.

### What I Built
A prompt-driven workflow that takes an existing resume + a target JD and returns:
- **ATS Match Score** — quick fit assessment
- **Gap Analysis** — missing keywords, missing skills, improvement opportunities
- **Optimized Resume** — same facts, reorganized and rephrased for ATS + recruiter relevance
- **Ready-to-use Word document** — clean, formatted, no manual editing needed

### Key Rule Enforced
> Never invent experience, employers, dates, metrics, or skills — only optimize, reorganize, and rephrase existing content.

### Takeaway
The real value isn't AI writing a "better" resume — it's AI correctly *mapping* your real experience onto the vocabulary a specific job description (and its ATS filter) is looking for.
