Workflow Summary

This workflow takes a small business from an unfocused posting habit to a repeatable, multi-platform promotion engine: a documented content strategy, a batch-production system for visuals/video/copy, a scheduling calendar spanning Instagram, LinkedIn and TikTok, active community engagement, and a monthly analytics loop that feeds back into what gets made next. Total build-out time for the first cycle is roughly 18–25 hours, dropping to 4–6 hours/week once the system is running.
