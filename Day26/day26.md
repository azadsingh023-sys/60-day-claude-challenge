Day 26: Prior Authorization Workflow Simulator

Built a single-file, gamified drag-and-drop simulator for the US 
healthcare Prior Authorization process.

- 3 workflow lanes: Patient, Provider, Payer
- Drag-and-drop case movement across stages
- 4 scenarios: elective surgery, MRI, specialty medication, inpatient admission
- Medical necessity evaluation + document collection checklist
- Outcomes: Approval, Pend, Denial, Appeal, Peer-to-Peer Review
- Progress tracker, days-elapsed counter, efficiency score
- Educational explanation after every step
- Celebration animation on approval + workflow summary

Tech: HTML, CSS, vanilla JS only — no frameworks, no CDNs, no localStorage.
