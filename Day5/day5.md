# 30-Day ITIL v4 Skill Enhancement Roadmap
**For:** ITSM/Service Desk professional, ITIL Foundation certified, intermediate level
**Time commitment:** 1 hour/day
**Style:** Videos + hands-on projects + reading

---

## How this roadmap works

You already have the ITIL v4 Foundation and years of hands-on incident/service desk experience — this plan isn't a re-intro. It goes deeper into the practices you use daily (Incident, Major Incident, Problem, Change, Service Desk) and builds toward ITIL v4 Specialist-level thinking, using your own tools (ServiceNow, M365/Azure AD, Intune) as the practice ground instead of generic examples.

Each day = ~20 min video/reading + ~40 min applied work (notes, templates, or a mini hands-on task). Weekends are lighter — for review and the week's project.

|---|---|---|
